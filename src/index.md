# jharg.com

A small collection of data-driven pieces on UK energy, built on top of an electricity pricing model I've been developing.

The model covers the full cost stack for domestic electricity — network charges, policy levies, wholesale, and retail — at half-hourly resolution for all 14 GB distribution regions.

---

## Energy

- [**Electricity pricing: overview**](./electricity-pricing/) — How the unit rate on your bill is built up: wholesale, network, policy levies, losses, and the formula that ties them together.
- [**DUoS: Distribution Use of System**](./duos/) — Explore the network charges that vary by region and time of day, built up from DNO charging statements going back to 2022.
- [**TNUoS: Transmission Network Use of System**](./tnuos/) — How transmission charges have evolved through TCR (2023) and RIIO-ET3 (2026), and what the official forecasts show.
- [**AAHEDC: High-cost distribution levy**](./aahedc/) — The flat national levy that cross-subsidises northern Scotland's distribution network.
