# jharg.com

A small collection of data-driven pieces on UK energy, built on top of an electricity pricing model I've been developing.

The model covers the full cost stack for domestic electricity — network charges, policy levies, wholesale, and retail — at half-hourly resolution for all 14 GB distribution regions.

## Energy

- [**DUoS: Distribution Use of System**](./duos/) — Explore the network charges that vary by region and time of day, built up from DNO charging statements going back to 2022.
